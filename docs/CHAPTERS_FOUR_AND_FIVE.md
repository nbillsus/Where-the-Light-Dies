# Chapters 4 and 5

Both chapters contain five side scrolling areas with distinct painted environments. Chapter 4 descends through the Sunken Archive, Bone Conduit, Mirror Well, Silent Engine, and Deep Transmitter. Chapter 5 returns through the Last Shore, Split Signal, Red Cathedral, Night Array, and Threshold.

The radio is Chapter 4's main new mechanic. Press `1`, `2`, or `3` to tune low, middle, or high. Low steadies unstable structures, middle reveals some hidden bridges, and high can draw enemies. At a radio console, press `E` once for each frequency shown in its sequence; a wrong note resets that console. Completed gates stay open and are saved. One console also needs the Shadow anchored in its prism. The older signal relay remains a separate puzzle: press `E` at its station, then anchor the Shadow with `F` in its receiver before the pulse fades.

| Chapter 4 area | Main challenge |
| --- | --- |
| Sunken Archive | Learn the radio code and reveal a middle-frequency bridge. |
| Bone Conduit | Stabilize a low-frequency bridge, hold a Shadow relay, and carry a relic to a plate. |
| Mirror Well | Anchor the Shadow in a prism while tuning a three-note radio lock; cross a light beam. |
| Silent Engine | Push a physical weight, tune the engine, and reveal a second bridge while high frequency attracts enemies. |
| Deep Transmitter | Combine a Shadow relay, two different radio sequences, and a dangerous beam. |

Chapter 5 deliberately changes its main interaction in each area:

| Area | Main challenge |
| --- | --- |
| Last Shore | Escape across collapsing shelves; low frequency gives more time, high makes the route break faster. |
| Split Signal | Push an ordinary weight, carry a Shadow relic, and reveal the bridge between them. |
| Red Cathedral | Tune a radio lock under high-frequency enemy pressure, silence a beam, hold one Shadow relay, and cross sustained light. |
| Night Array | Tune a three-note radio lock, capture light in a reflection lens to open a gate, then release the Shadow and silence the final beam. High frequency draws enemies. |
| Threshold | Combine a relay, radio lock, both weight types, a beam, and the final Shadow lens before the boss. |

The Threshold gate only opens after its relay, radio, and weight conditions are met.

The Chapter 3 Undertow boss now marks two strike zones before each thunderstorm. Lightning damages the Keeper during the strike window and briefly exposes the boss's eye. Storms come faster in its second phase. Its existing floor surge remains active, so the player must dodge both attacks.

Skitterers and Shroud Wardens appear across both chapters. Beyond the Threshold is a continuous three-chamber final encounter: the Drowned Crown, Iron Choir, and Last Eclipse. Each form has distinct art, attack timing, health, and color. The health bar refills during each transformation, and the Keeper regains all five hearts after clearing a chamber. Dying in any chamber resets all three forms and returns the Keeper to the first arena entrance. Defeating the Last Eclipse triggers an explosion, blackout, and scrolling credits for Neel, Lucas, and Axel.

Run `node scripts/generate_deep_chapters_art.mjs` after changing platform coordinates or painted environment layouts.
