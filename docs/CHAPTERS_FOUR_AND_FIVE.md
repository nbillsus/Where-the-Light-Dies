# Chapters 4 and 5

Both chapters contain five side scrolling areas with distinct painted environments. Chapter 4 descends through the Sunken Archive, Bone Conduit, Mirror Well, Silent Engine, and Deep Transmitter. Chapter 5 returns through the Last Shore, Split Signal, Red Cathedral, Night Array, and Threshold.

The new signal relay mechanic uses `E` at a station to release a pulse. Before it fades, move the Shadow to the nearby receiver and press `F` to anchor it. This opens the corresponding gate permanently. Chapter 4 introduces the relay and combines it with grasp, pressure plates, and dangerous beams. Chapter 5 shortens the pulse window and layers several relays with grasp, beams, enemy patrols, and a final Shadow lens. The Threshold gate only opens after its relay and weight conditions are met.

Skitterers and Shroud Wardens appear across both chapters. Chapter 5 ends at the open Threshold, with a story card that leaves the final boss for a later design decision.

Run `node scripts/generate_deep_chapters_art.mjs` after changing platform coordinates or painted environment layouts.
