# Chapters 4 and 5

Both chapters contain five side scrolling areas with distinct painted environments. Chapter 4 descends through the Sunken Archive, Bone Conduit, Mirror Well, Silent Engine, and Deep Transmitter. Chapter 5 returns through the Last Shore, Split Signal, Red Cathedral, Night Array, and Threshold.

The new signal relay mechanic uses `E` at a station to release a pulse. Before it fades, move the Shadow to the nearby receiver and press `F` to anchor it. This opens the corresponding gate permanently. Chapter 4 introduces the relay and combines it with grasp, pressure plates, and dangerous beams.

Chapter 5 deliberately changes its main interaction in each area:

| Area | Main challenge |
| --- | --- |
| Last Shore | A timed escape over three collapsing coastal shelves, with no relay puzzle. |
| Split Signal | Push an ordinary weight as the Keeper, then carry a different weight with the Shadow. |
| Red Cathedral | Silence one beam, time a single signal relay, and cross a second sustained beam. |
| Night Array | Navigate three differently timed light barriers by anchoring the Shadow at their receivers. |
| Threshold | Combine two signal relays, both weight types, a beam, and the last Shadow lens. |

The Threshold gate only opens after its relay and weight conditions are met.

Skitterers and Shroud Wardens appear across both chapters. Chapter 5 ends at the open Threshold, with a story card that leaves the final boss for a later design decision.
